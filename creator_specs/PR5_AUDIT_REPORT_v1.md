## Correction from v2.0 — inconsistent exemptions, still-partial ledger, caught and fixed

v2.0 fixed the arithmetic and counting-unit errors from v1.0, but Codex round 2 found it still applied ad hoc, inconsistent exemptions rather than one uniform rule:
- "Calendar fact" was used to exempt a Can-Chi-to-Ngũ-Hành element mapping (a traditional classification claim, not pure date arithmetic).
- "Classification, this IS the symbol" was used to exempt a flatly-stated hexagram-symbol attribution, while an equivalent flatly-stated zodiac-compatibility classification elsewhere in the same report was correctly counted — inconsistent.
- A softening-degree word ("gần như") was treated as an adequate hedge in one script while an equivalent word ("nhìn chung") was correctly *not* treated as adequate in another — inconsistent.
- "Reporting the computed source-data answer" was used to exempt a sentence that directly answers a fortune-framed question ("which sign is luckiest") — but answering that question *is* the belief-claim, not a neutral data lookup.
- "Already counted in an earlier sentence" was used to skip a sentence with its own independent unhedged claim — contradicts the declared per-sentence counting unit.
- Two imperative/advisory sentences were marked neutral (`—`) without adjudication, when their premise carries an unstated belief-claim — Codex correctly required at minimum a `DISPUTED` marking, not a silent pass.

**This revision applies one uniform rule, with no per-sentence carve-outs for "it's just naming a classification" or "it's already implied elsewhere":** any independent sentence presenting a traditional/mystical relationship, mechanism, symbol-attribution, or status as fact — without a hedge word ("theo quan niệm/truyền thống," "được xem là," "có thể," "thường," or equivalent register-level softening, not just degree-softening like "gần như"/"nhìn chung") — counts as a `(d)` violation, independently, regardless of whether an adjacent sentence also touches the same topic. Every sentence in every script is adjudicated individually below, including ones marked compliant — some quotes are excerpted with "..." for brevity where the omitted portion doesn't affect the adjudication, but no sentence-unit is skipped, summarized as a group, or left unadjudicated (corrected per Codex round 3, which noted "listed" could be misread as "verbatim").
